The Process

From Messy to Mission-Ready

A structured, repeatable 5-step workflow that guarantees data quality at every stage.

01

Data Analysis

Full audit of the raw dataset — structure review, column mapping, anomaly detection, and completeness scoring.

02

Error Detection

Identify duplicates, invalid emails, formatting errors, missing values, inconsistent naming, and data type mismatches.

03

Validation & Cleanup

Systematic correction of every flagged issue. Emails validated, names standardized, currencies normalized, duplicates removed.

04

Audit Reporting

Detailed cleaning report: rows in/out, cells changed, duplication rate, email analysis, completeness by column.

05

Optimized Delivery

Final clean dataset delivered with the full audit trail, formatting guide, and optional integration-ready formatting.
