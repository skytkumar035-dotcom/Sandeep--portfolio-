FAQ

Common Questions

What file formats do you accept? +
I accept .xlsx, .csv, .xls, and Google Sheets exports. I can also work with .ods and tab-delimited files. For CRM data, I work with HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho, and Pipedrive exports.
How do I know what changes were made? +
Every project includes a full cleaning audit report that documents rows in/out, cells changed, duplicates removed, emails validated, and field-level changes. You get full transparency on every modification.
Is my data kept confidential? +
Absolutely. All client data is treated with strict confidentiality. I can sign an NDA before starting if your project requires it. Files are not shared, stored, or used for any purpose outside of the project.
What CRM systems are supported? +
I work with exports from any CRM — including HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho CRM, Pipedrive, Monday.com, Airtable, and others. Deliverables are formatted for re-import into your specific CRM if needed.
Do you offer revisions? +
Yes. All projects include at least one free revision. If anything doesn't meet the agreed scope, I'll fix it promptly. Client satisfaction is the core of every delivery.
