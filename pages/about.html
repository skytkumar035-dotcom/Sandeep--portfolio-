Professional Background & Expertise

I am a data operations specialist focused on business data cleaning, CRM optimization, and lead database management. My work primarily serves sales agencies, SaaS companies, outreach teams, and operations managers who need reliable, structured data to drive their business processes.

What sets this practice apart is a systematic, auditable approach to every project. Rather than delivering a cleaned file without documentation, every engagement includes a full data quality audit report — tracking rows in, rows out, cells changed, email validation results, and completeness scoring by column.

This methodology was built after processing thousands of messy CRM exports, lead databases, and outreach lists. The result is a repeatable 5-step workflow that consistently delivers 96%+ data accuracy with zero guesswork on what changed and why.

I operate as a solo specialist with deep focus on data quality, not a generalist agency. This means faster turnaround, clearer communication, and a single point of accountability for every project.
